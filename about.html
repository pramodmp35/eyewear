Our journey

Fifteen years, one obsession

From a single workbench in Brooklyn to 128 studios worldwide — tap any milestone to explore the moments that shaped STACKLY.

2010

A single workbench in Brooklyn

STACKLY is founded by two opticians frustrated by overpriced frames. The first collection — nine hand-cut acetate models — sells out in three weeks from a small studio on Wythe Avenue.

2013

Our first flagship opens in SoHo

A 1,200 sq ft studio on Grand Street becomes the brand's home. The in-store lens lab means customers can walk out with finished prescription glasses in under an hour.

2016

Going global — London & Tokyo

Our first two international flagships open within a month of each other. Local design teams adapt the core collection to regional fit preferences, and the "one frame, many faces" ethos is born.

2019

The bio-acetate breakthrough

After two years of R&D, we launch our first bio-acetate frame — made from renewable cotton seed and wood pulp. It cuts production carbon by 42% with zero compromise on feel.

2022

STACKLY Recycles is born

We launch a global frame take-back programme. Every returned frame is separated, cleaned and re-ground into new sheet acetate. To date we've recovered 82 tonnes of material.

2025

128 studios, 42 countries, one mission

STACKLY now employs over 900 people and serves more than 250,000 customers a year. The mission hasn't changed: honest eyewear, built to last, priced fairly.

What drives us

Vision & mission

Two statements that guide every frame we make, every studio we open and every decision in between.

Our vision

A world where great eyewear is the norm, not the exception

We want to make premium, long-lasting frames accessible to anyone who needs them — not just those who can afford a designer markup.

  • Accessible pricing, always
  • Sustainable materials as standard
  • A studio within reach of every city

Our mission

To craft eyewear that lasts as long as the people who wear it

Every STACKLY frame passes through 42 hand-finished stages, using materials chosen for durability and comfort — not the lowest bid.

  • Hand-finished in-house
  • 2-year warranty on every frame
  • Free repairs for life
