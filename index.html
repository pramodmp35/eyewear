Our story

Fifteen years of getting eyewear right

STACKLY started with two opticians, one workbench, and a stubborn belief that a great pair of glasses shouldn't cost a month's rent. Today we make frames for 250,000 people a year — and we still finish every single one by hand.

Honest materials Bio-acetate, titanium and recycled metals only.
Fair pricing No designer markup. Ever.

Every product page includes the frame's width, bridge, temple length and lens height. If you already own a pair that fits well, compare those numbers. Or book a free 20-minute fitting at any studio — our opticians will measure you properly.

Manufacturing defects, hinge failures, acetate cracking and coating delamination — all covered. Accidental damage and normal wear are not, but we do offer free tune-ups and adjustments for the life of the frame at any studio.
